# Slumbot session for v4.1.0 (27 Nov 2026)

**Question:** how does today's live bot (v4.1.0: preflop realization 0.85, opponent stats) do against Slumbot, and where does it leak? Same tool and format as the [25 Nov baseline](2026-11-25-slumbot-baseline.md).

**What and how**
- **Build:** main at 14f915b, engine built with `tools/build_engine.sh`; `build_info()` reported `git_sha 14f915b, preflop_realization 0.85, call_narrow 1.0`.
- **Hero:** `chipzen/bot.py` through the bench View (`--hero bot`), 5000 sims (the live setting), seed 1128.
- **Game:** anonymous session, blinds 50/100, 200 bb stacks reset every hand, seats as the API deals them. One request at a time, 0.2 s between hands, at most two retries. Latency about 0.11 s per request (p95 0.12 s). 700 s for 1,000 hands.
- **Unpaired:** the deals are random, so the interval comes from the per-hand standard deviation (2,507 chips).
- **Checks:** a 20-hand smoke (0 rejected, 0 mismatches), then the main run: 0 rejected actions and 0 reconcile mismatches over 4,405 requests. The log holds no session token.
- **Analysis:** `results/2026-11-27-slumbot-scripts/report.py` on `results/slumbot-logs/2026-11-27-bot-v410-5000sims-1000.jsonl`.

**Caveats**
- **Not like for like:** 5000 vs 2000 sims, a different build (preflop realization 0.85 and opponent stats are new; the opponent stats are logged only and change no play), and unpaired random deals.
- **Samples too thin:** all three intervals overlap, and the 27 Nov interval includes zero. The difference between the rows cannot be read as real. The two 25 Nov runs (the same play code, two commits) differed by 300 bb/100 on their own.
- **Preflop shape differs on its face** (no preflop folds, 89% of the big blind's opens called on 25 Nov against 99% now), but those are counts from different builds and different sims; no claim about cause.
- **Depth and opponent:** Slumbot plays 200 bb deep and every hand resets, so this shows leaks, not adaptation. Chipzen games start at 100 bb.
- **Local only:** `tools/slumbot.py` is not in CI; it calls an outside service.
